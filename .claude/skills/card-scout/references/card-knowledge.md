# Card knowledge for this watch list

Short, opinionated notes on the products and players the watch list covers. Read the section
that matches the search before pulling comps, because the biggest comp mistakes come from
mixing two things that look alike in a title.

## Grade buckets (never mix them)

| Bucket | Rough multiple of raw | Watch-list relevance |
|---|---|---|
| PSA 10 | 3x to 8x | "giannis antetokounmpo rookie psa 10" |
| BGS 9.5 | 2x to 5x | BGS 10 and Black Label are far above PSA 10 |
| PSA 9 | 1x to 2x | close to a clean raw on modern cards |
| SGC 10 | 1.5x to 3x | "larry bird sgc": the market still discounts SGC against PSA, which is the opportunity |
| CSG / CGC 10 | raw to 1.5x | "csg basketball", "csg luka doncic", "giannis antetokounmpo csg", "larry bird csg": the nephew is clearly buying CSG slabs at near-raw prices. A CSG 10 comp is not a PSA 10 comp. |
| Raw | 1x | price in the gamble of grading |

A title with "PSA 10?" or "gem mint" and no slab in the photo is a raw card.

## 2013-14 Panini Prizm (Giannis rookie year, Prizm's second year)

The list has seven separate searches for this set, so it matters. Order of value for the
Giannis #290, low to high: base, Red/White/Blue (retail, no serial), Blue /199, Red /125,
Purple /49, Orange /60 (actually /60, not /99, sellers get this wrong), Green /25,
Gold /10, Black 1/1. "Prizm" with no colour named and a rainbow sheen in the photo is a
Silver, which is the chase card and often 5x to 10x base.

Sellers drop the colour constantly. A "2013-14 Prizm Giannis" listing at a base price whose
photo shows a blue border is a deal; one whose photo shows a silver sheen is a big deal. Always
look at the photo before calling anything.

## Topps Chrome and Finest refractors (1998 to 2009)

Refractor family, low to high: Refractor (unnumbered in most years), X-Fractor (numbered in
some years, e.g. 2007-08 /50 ... check the year), Gold Refractor (/99 in 2003-09 era),
Black Refractor (/99 in 2002-03 and 2008-09), Red /25, Superfractor 1/1. Topps Finest
(2005-06) has Refractor, Gold /29 (hence the "2005 finest gold /29" search), Black /99.

The tell in the photo is the rainbow sheen; a base chrome card has a plain mirror finish.
Sellers list refractors as "chrome" all the time because they don't know, and that is the
whole reason these set-wide searches exist. Scan photos of cheap "topps chrome" listings for
the sheen; scan "refractor" listings for a serial number the title didn't mention.

## Court Kings (Panini, 2013 onward)

Rookie tiers are the trap: Level 1 is common, Level 2 and Level 3 are scarcer, Level 4 is
the short print and the one the list wants ("court kings level 4", "giannis antetokounmpo
rookie court kings"). A title saying "Court Kings rookie" with no level is Level 1 until the
photo proves otherwise. Inserts on the list: Aurora, Dieci Migliore (Italian for "top ten"),
Swagger. Sellers misspell Dieci Migliore in every way imaginable, so typo trawl it.

## Donruss Optic (2016-17 is the first year)

Holo is the Optic version of Silver Prizm. "Optic Holo Dimes" is a 2016-17 insert. "White
Sparkle" is a Fanatics/Panini Direct exclusive and sells far above Holo. "Fast Break" is
the Fanatics retail parallel with its own look (disco pattern). A 2016-17 Optic Giannis titled
just "Optic" with a holo sheen in the photo is a buried Holo.

## Select, Courtside tier

Select has Concourse (common), Premier, Courtside (short print). "Courtside silver" and
"rookie courtside select" are both after the Courtside tier Silver Prizm. Sellers often title
a Concourse Silver as just "Select Silver" and price it the same; the reverse (a Courtside
titled as "Select") is the deal.

## Autos and patches

On-card auto beats sticker auto, 1.5x to 3x. "Panini auto" searches for Giannis will mix
both; split comps by reading titles and photos. "Khris middleton patch auto" and "ray allen
bucks auto": patch quality (multi-colour, logo) moves the price more than the serial.
"Facsimile auto" is a printed signature, excluded by default. "Clipboard signatures bill
sharman" is a cut-signature product; comps are thin, so expect low confidence.

## Vintage Bird and oddballs

"1989 converse larry bird" is an oddball promo issue; comps are scarce and condition is
everything. "2006-07 topps larry bird 33" is a Topps base card #33 (the list also has a
graded-only search for it); these sell for little raw and more as PSA 9/10, so grade bucket
discipline matters more than usual.

## Sealed product

"2016-17 optic basketball hobby box", "2018-19 optic fast break box", "2018-19 basketball
pack". Resealed boxes are the danger. Prefer sellers with long sealed-product history, photos
of the factory seal, and no "may have been searched" language. Price moves slowly, so comps
from 90 days back are fine.

## Red flags in any title

`reprint`, `rp`, `facsimile`, `custom`, `novelty`, `aceo`, `proxy`: not the card.
`lot`, `bundle`, `you pick`, `break`: not a single card; see the lot lens in SKILL.md.
`as is`, `crease`, `oc`, `surface`, `dinged`, `wax stain`: damage disclosed, usually a trap.
A grader not in the table above (GMA, HGA, TAG, ISA, BCCG): treat as raw.
