# Watch list

Transcribed from the eBay saved-search screenshots. Each line is one saved search, exactly as
typed on eBay (lowercase). The number in parentheses is the "new listings" badge at the time of
the screenshot; it is only a hint about how busy that search is, not a target.

Rows marked `(gap?)` sit at the boundary of a screenshot, so there may be saved searches
between them that have not been transcribed yet. Add new rows in alphabetical order, the same
way eBay sorts them.

## How to read a row

`search text | player | focus | notes`

- **player**: who the search is really about, even when the text doesn't say (e.g. "bucks refractor" is a Giannis hunt).
- **focus**: `card` (one specific card or parallel), `set` (a whole set or parallel run), `sealed` (box or pack), `player` (anything of that player).
- **notes**: numbering (`/99`), grade, or why it's interesting. Blank when the search text says it all.

## Players this list cares about

- **Giannis Antetokounmpo** (Milwaukee Bucks, 2013 rookie). The heart of the list. Any "bucks" search is almost certainly about him.
- **Larry Bird** (Boston Celtics). Vintage and retro issues.
- **Luka Doncic** (2018 rookie). Graded and parallel rookies.
- **Bill Sharman** (Celtics, 1950s). Cut signatures only.
- **Bucks role players**: Khris Middleton, Brook Lopez, Ryan Rollins, Ray Allen (Bucks era). Autos and inserts.
- **One-offs**: Kobe Bryant (Winner Stays), Stephen Curry (silver prizm), Walker Kessler (Prizm rookie #234).

## Standing exclusions

Unless the user asks for them, drop listings whose titles contain:
`reprint`, `rp`, `custom`, `novelty`, `digital`, `nft`, `facsimile`, `proxy`, `aceo`, `art card`,
`sticker`, `break` (as in "you get what you pull"), `mystery`, `repack`.
